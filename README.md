# WealthTheShaper — Business Growth & Consultancy Platform

A premium, database-driven business consultancy website featuring client authentication, booking management, customer dashboards, review moderation, and dynamic website content management.

## About the Project

WealthTheShaper is a professional business growth and commercial strategy platform designed to help brands improve their positioning, visibility, marketing, and sales opportunities.

The website combines a premium frontend experience with a Supabase-powered backend to support client interactions, booking workflows, reviews, and website content management.

## Key Features

### Premium Frontend Design
- Modern, professional interface with responsive layouts.
- Dedicated sections for business services, about information, completed projects, and frequently asked questions.
- Integrated social media links and a professionally designed footer.
- Dynamic website content managed through the backend rather than hardcoded page content.

### Client Authentication & Dashboard
- Client registration and authentication.
- Personalized client dashboard.
- Booking history and booking status tracking.
- Visibility into bookings awaiting completion by the administrator.

### Booking Management System
- Clients can submit booking requests through the website.
- Administrators can review and accept or reject booking requests.
- Clients can track their bookings through their dashboard.
- Booking status updates support the service management workflow.

### Client Review System
- Clients can submit reviews with star ratings after their bookings have been completed.
- Submitted reviews require administrator approval or rejection.
- Approved reviews can be displayed publicly on the website.

### Admin Dashboard & Content Management
- Centralized administration interface.
- Management of booking requests and booking statuses.
- Review moderation and approval workflows.
- Dynamic management of website content.
- Database-driven content for supported sections, including FAQs and other website information.

## Technology Stack

- **HTML5** — Website structure.
- **CSS3** — Styling, responsive layouts, and visual presentation.
- **JavaScript** — Frontend interactions and application logic.
- **Supabase** — Database integration, authentication, and backend functionality.

## Technical Highlights

- Integration between frontend interfaces and backend data.
- Authentication for client-facing features.
- Separate client and administrator workflows.
- Database-driven website content.
- Booking lifecycle and review moderation workflows.
- Dynamic rendering of supported content.

## Project Purpose

This project demonstrates the development of a business consultancy platform that goes beyond a static marketing website. It combines a premium public-facing website with authenticated client features and administrative tools.

It showcases practical experience in frontend development, backend integration, database-driven applications, dashboard development, and workflow management.
